Privacy & Terms

Simple, transparent, and respectful.

We collect only what you choose to share when you contact us, and we use it to fulfill your apple requests and keep in touch.

What we collect

We only collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, order details, pickup preference, and any notes you include when you email us.

How we use it

We use your information to confirm orders, coordinate pickup, and answer questions. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Cookies & tracking

Our site does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Data retention

We keep order-related emails for our records. If you want your messages deleted from our inbox, email us at enrightsapples@gmail.com.

Pricing & products

Pricing may change; handmade products can vary slightly in appearance. We do our best to honor the flavors and quantities you request.

External links

Our site links to Instagram and Facebook. Those sites have their own privacy policies and terms. Please review them before sharing information there.

Questions? Reach us at enrightsapples@gmail.com. We’re happy to clarify anything here.
